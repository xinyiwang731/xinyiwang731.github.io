About Me

Hi! I'm Xinyi, a Ph.D. candidate in Communication at the Annenberg School for Communication, University of Pennsylvania. My research sits at the intersection of health communication, behavioral science, and computational methods.

I study how people seek and process health information — with a focus on tobacco control, health interventions, and experience sampling methodology (ESM). I hold an M.A. in Statistics and Data Science from the Wharton School and an M.A. and B.A. in Journalism and Mass Communication from the University of Wisconsin-Madison.
