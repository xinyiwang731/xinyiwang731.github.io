My research sits at the intersection of message effects, information seeking, and curiosity-driven mechanisms.

I'm a Postdoctoral Research Fellow and a Penn Climate Fellow at the University of Pennsylvania .

My research investigates:
1) how curiosity-driven mechanisms shape information elaboration.
2) how information elaboration processes can inform the design of messaging to enhance persuasion.
3) how do people seek information in daily life, online, and through AI.
